// architecture

How I Build Systems

🏗️

Infrastructure as Code, Always

Every resource is declared in Terraform or Pulumi. Environments are disposable and reproducible. Modules are versioned, reviewed, and tested before promotion. No click-ops in production.

🔄

GitOps-First Delivery

Git is the single source of truth for cluster state. ArgoCD reconciles continuously. Deployments are PR-driven with automated rollback on SLO breach — no manual kubectl in prod.

🔐

Security Baked In, Not Bolted On

Shift-left security: SAST, SCA, and image scanning in CI. OPA/Gatekeeper enforces policy at admission. Zero-trust networking with mTLS (Istio/Linkerd). Vault for dynamic secrets — no static credentials anywhere.

📊

Observe Before You Operate

Metrics, logs, and traces are non-negotiable from day one. SLOs are defined before launch. Alerts are actionable, not noisy. Every runbook is codified and linked from the alert.

⚙️

Platform Thinking

I build platforms, not snowflakes. Self-service templates, golden paths, and guardrails let product teams move fast without breaking things. The platform team is a product team.

🤖

MLOps as a First-Class Discipline

Models are software. They need versioning, testing, staged rollouts, monitoring, and rollback. I bring the same rigor to ML pipelines as to any other production workload — feature stores, model registries, drift detection, and A/B serving included.
